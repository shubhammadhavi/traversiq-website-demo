US F1 Student & B1/B2 Visitor Preparation

Honest, structured preparation for your US Consulate interview.

A US visa interview at the Mumbai BKC Consulate typically lasts under two minutes. Refusals under Section 214(b) frequently occur when the DS-160 contains incomplete one-line descriptions, omits mandatory disclosures, or when spoken answers sound memorized.

We work with you to ensure your DS-160, genuine financial documents, and spoken answers are clear and consistent. Strict Integrity Policy: We only organize and present genuine, verifiable applicant documents—we never fabricate, inflate, or alter financial or employment records.

How We Prepare Your US Application (3 Steps)

STEP 01

Line-by-Line DS-160 & Social Media Compliance

Consular officers review your DS-160 before asking the first question. We help you clearly articulate your work duties, study plans, and travel history, and verify compliance with the mandatory 5-year social media username disclosure & public profile requirement for F, M, and J student/exchange applicants.

STEP 02

Organizing Genuine Financial & Home-Ties Documents

Whether you are presenting a sanctioned education loan, parental sponsorship, or business/salary ITRs, we help you organize your genuine paperwork into a clear, easy-to-verify folder.

STEP 03

Realistic Mock Counter Interviews

Practical counter drills covering your university choice, curriculum, sponsor's profession, or visit purpose so you communicate naturally and concisely without memorized scripts.
